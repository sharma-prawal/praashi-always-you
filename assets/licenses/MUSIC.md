## Changes made

Both tracks (each estimated Bb major) were trimmed of lead-in silence, levelled to about -18 LUFS, joined with a 4 s crossfade, given a 3 s fade-in and 4 s fade-out plus 0.4 s of silence so the loop has no click, then encoded as 112 kbps MP3 with ffmpeg. No melody was altered.

No vocals. Selected on licence and measured properties (tempo, dynamics, key). The audio has been measured, not listened to by the implementer: the owner should listen once before release.
