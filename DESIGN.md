# A keepsake under the stars

The user has delegated design decisions in a detailed brief. This build follows those commitments directly without an interview or design approval flow.

## Direction
Experience mode. Seven grounded material directions considered: a handwritten love letter; a private cinema; a celestial keepsake album; a pressed-flower book; a jeweller's locket; a dusk garden; a ribbon-bound folio. The seeded third direction is the celestial keepsake album, already grounded by the requested constellation. The concept helper ran without remote challengers. No generated face or stock photograph is used.

## Visual system
Midnight plum #1C1124 and a slightly darker surrounding stage. Ivory #FFF8EF, muted blush #F7CAD7 and champagne #E9C47A. Self-hosted Cormorant Garamond for lyrical headings and DM Sans for controls. Real photographs in warm ivory mounted frames, fine orbital linework, sparse stars, sculpted envelope and glowing heart. No dense cards, emoji decorations or floating-heart wallpaper. The supplied letter's emoji are preserved.

### Approved creative-direction refinements
The P–A monogram accompanies the readable header wordmark. Local SVG assets in public/assets supply a low-opacity nebula and star backdrop, light filaments during the opening transition, layered heart linework, and sunlight/mist in the weather portal. They remain decorative and do not intercept gestures or add accessible names. The paper SVG is applied as a stretchable border image with fixed-size gold corners, a lightly textured centre and subtle irregular paper edges; it must never reduce text contrast or clip letter content. The complete supplied letter remains unchanged. The finale displays the guide's exact final promise beneath the photographic constellation. All art is cached with the offline shell. Avoid adding more continuous motion or stacking decorative effects over the photographs.

## Composition
One centered intimate stage, maximum 520px, on a quiet desktop night field. Six original individually composed scenes plus the inserted 2021 letter chapter make seven chapters. A sticky, opaque header keeps the wordmark and promise/audio actions reachable during long reading. Generous serif typography, photo stacks and deliberate empty space. Main keepsake prints use ivory mattes to preserve full photographic compositions. The collection balances two columns of naturally proportioned prints; its full-screen viewer contains every image and supports swipe, arrows and keyboard navigation. One bottom chapter rail provides orientation, direct access, an explicit Back action and a chapter count. On short phones content scrolls naturally; no fixed content clips the letter. The ending is a compact viewport composition with its primary and replay actions visible on a normal phone, with natural scrolling retained at the smallest size.

### 2021 letter chapter
“Where It All Began” extends the existing envelope, ivory paper and serif language after the weather scene. Its subtitle is “It started with a good morning.” Three featured envelopes open into selectable excerpts; previous/next controls accompany touch swipes. Swiping yields to reading and page zoom. Paper carries verified greetings when present, an excerpt and a route to its original screenshot. Opening an envelope transfers keyboard focus to the excerpt; the next Tab reaches “View original” (Option-Tab with Safari's default keyboard-navigation setting). Brief paper-edge and opacity reveals use the existing motion language, with five finite decorative motes and no timed navigation gate; reduced motion presents the settled state.

The optional ten-fragment archive uses a chronological paper timeline in a native dialog within the same narrow stage. Its opaque sticky header retains Back and Close controls while the content scrolls. The original viewer first shows a closer display window onto the authentic note pixels, omitting embedded phone chrome and ending between text lines. “View complete screenshot” reveals the byte-identical source PNG at its full proportions; native page zoom remains available. Failed images offer retry with the excerpt still available. Returning restores the relevant reading position and focus; closing restores the story position and opening control, and reopening retains the collection position. Featured/opened envelopes and the bridge persist across reloads until replay. The quiet typographic bridge uses the supplied lines to enter the original heart interaction; an additional Then/Now sequence would repeat it. No archive-specific paper shade or decorative detail establishes a new system token; source dates and text live in app data rather than this design guide.

## Motion
First entry: light expands into the photographic chapter. Scene exits/entrances use a 220ms opacity crossfade. Full-scene transforms are deliberately avoided so WebKit does not retain a transformed, full-height reading layer. Scroll and heading focus reset only when the next scene mounts; no scene transform is retained. Photographs use uniquely keyed, bounded spring transitions so rapid wraparound cannot strand an outgoing frame. The finale gathers six prints towards their final orbital positions in a single, sub-second gesture. Stars use only sparse CSS opacity animation, paused while reading or when the page is hidden. Hold response lasts two seconds and cancels on matching-pointer release/cancel, focus loss or a hidden page. The long letter uses a brief opacity reveal rather than a large 3D layer; 16px body text stays immediately readable. Reduced motion removes decorative motion, preserves hold progress and direct user swiping, and uses brief fades. No continuous canvas loop or GSAP required.

## Accessibility
Minimum 44px controls. Visible ivory focus ring. Actual buttons for stars and native range for weather. Native dialog for gallery/lightbox with focus management. Tap alternative to holding, no timed unlocks. Body text is selectable; zoom enabled; no sound until explicit opt-in.

## Mobile refinement

Use dynamic viewport height and safe-area variables backed by env() insets. The constellation has 56px circular targets, stationary readable labels, sparse star twinkle, a finite connection trace and a collection alternative. The hold uses a quicker heartbeat and live progress wording; keyboard activation opens the letter directly. Only a small decorative paper fold animates above the readable sheet. Three existing promises receive typographic emphasis without changing text. The ending softens its background, gathers the photographs, then reveals the heading and three exact promise clauses over about 1.7 seconds. All controls remain usable throughout. Reduced motion presents the settled composition immediately. Gallery drag surfaces persist through photo fades and yield to native pinch zoom; close restores document scroll and focus synchronously.

Visible-viewport dimensions supplement dynamic CSS units for Safari toolbar changes. Update only on viewport events, coalesce work into one frame, and retain the existing layout during native pinch zoom. Modal bounds follow the visible viewport; document content continues scrolling naturally, with safe bottom spacing on every chapter rail. Programmatically focused reading headings remain visually quiet; interactive controls and excerpt focus retain their existing focus treatment.

## 2021 envelope and soundtrack

The envelope is layered paper, not an image: lining, folded note, pocket, then a translucent flap with its seal. Opening order is seal press, flap back, note rises, reading page grows out of the note, envelope settles away, about 1.1 s in all, transform and opacity only. Reduced motion opens immediately. Music is one self-hosted file through one player; chapter changes only adjust its level, quieter for the letters and promise, fuller at the ending.
