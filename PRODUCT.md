# #PrAashi — Always You

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
User specified React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Motion, static JSON, localStorage, PWA support and Playwright.

## Users
Nikki, receiving a private romantic gift from her husband Pawi (Prawal), primarily on a modern phone.

## Product Purpose
A complete interactive promise: through every high and low, Pawi will always choose Nikki. The six original scenes are preserved, with one 2021 letter chapter inserted after the weather scene and before the promise, making seven chapters.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Opening invitation, photo keepsakes, tappable constellation, interactive symbolic weather, an optional archive of authentic 2021 message fragments, accessible hold-to-reveal exact letter, finale, replay, gallery, silent-by-default optional licensed piano soundtrack, local progress, offline support. The letter chapter, “Where It All Began”, leads through three featured envelopes or ten chronological fragments to the supplied bridge into the existing promise; the header gallery opens after all seven stars, and the promise shortcut after one complete run. No accounts, backend, analytics, remote photo requests or invented biographical details. Originals in Photos remain untouched.

## Brand Commitments
Name #PrAashi — Always You. Subtitle: A little world made just for Nikki. Cinematic intimacy, quiet luxury, warmth and wonder. Midnight plum, blush, champagne and ivory. Exact supplied letter preserved.

## Evidence on Hand
Personal photographs in Photos/ recursively, including Milestones/. Ten supplied 2021 message screenshots and their manifest support the letter archive; excerpts and dates remain in the generated app data, with provenance and import behavior recorded in LETTERS-INTEGRATION.md. Screenshots are presented as fragments, never reconstructed full letters. No claims about dates, locations or events will be derived from filenames or images.

## Product Principles
The photographs and promise lead. All emotional content remains directly accessible. Touch first; desktop stays intimate. Minimal local-only data. No approval or content questions: the user's detailed brief explicitly delegates creative and technical choices.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
320px and larger, safe areas, reduced motion, readable selectable letter, keyboard alternatives, 44px touch targets, zoom enabled.
